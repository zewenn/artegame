# Artegame v4.0.0 Specification

Artegame v4.0.0 overhauls the project's core mechanics. We will introduce **a tutorial**, a **new boon system**, **room-based progression**, **more enemies** (including dynamically scaling mini-bosses and bosses), a **higher enemy cap**, a **tile-based background/map system**, and **quality-of-life (QoL) improvements**.

## Room-Based Progression

Each round will have its own *room*; this results in a more dynamic experience and gives the player more control when a round ends.

### Gameplay

When the player enters a room, the round state will immediately be set to `.combat`. When all enemies are defeated, room rewards will be granted.
After the round is finished and `roundState` is set to `.replenish`, the player will have the chance to choose from one or more doors leading to subsequent rooms. Each door will feature an icon indicating the reward type granted once that room is completed.

#### Boon Doors

Boon-specific doors — and the rooms they lead to — give the player greater control over their progression. Rather than hoping for a desired augmentation from an overwhelming pool of boons, players can target the exact upgrade categories they need.
Boon door rewards — and where they lead — are randomized after each round finishes, preventing players from banking on a predictable or overpowered path.
Boon doors lead to rooms granting a specific category of boons selected from that category's pool. For example, if a player enters a "Plate Upgrade" door, upon completing the room, the offered rewards will only come from that designated category.

#### Boon Categories

Boon categories should be stored as a struct, like this:
```zig
const BoonCategory = struct {
    name: []const u8,
    icon: []const u8,
    boons: []Boon,
};
```
The categories should be stored in a list, and each door receives a random item from that list. This `BoonCategory` struct is attached to the next room. When the round is completed, the player is presented with a choice of boons — using the existing 3-card selection UI — with all options drawn from the room's designated category. As before, **only one boon can be chosen from the selection**.

### Mini-Boss Rooms

Every 5 rooms, a **mini-boss room** appears. After clearing 4 regular rooms, whichever door the player chooses will lead to a mini-boss. After defeating the mini-boss, the player's HP is fully restored to maximum, and they permanently gain +10% max HP.

### Boss Rooms

Every 15 rooms, a **boss room** appears. After clearing 14 rooms, whichever door the player chooses will lead to a boss. After defeating the boss, the player's HP is fully restored to maximum, and they permanently gain +15% max HP, +15 physical damage, and +10 magic damage.

## New Enemies & Changes

In Artegame v4.0.0, many new enemies will be introduced. These will arrive alongside structural changes to the projectile system, enemy ability handling, and spawning logic.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The enemy cap will be raised from 128 to 256 per room.

### Normal Enemies

Normal enemies spawn in every room except mini-boss and boss rooms.

#### Shaman

The Shaman is an enemy type that buffs, heals, and summons other enemies.

##### Abilities

 - **Remote healing**: The Shaman sends out pass-through projectiles (8 projectiles, every 45 degrees) that heal allies by a set amount, scaling with the current room number (round). (medium cooldown)
 - **Speed Boost**: The Shaman targets an ally enemy and grants them a random movement speed increase. (short cooldown)
 - **Grieving Wounds**: A fast-traveling, narrow projectile. If it hits the player, all currently active enemies are healed for the amount of damage dealt. (long cooldown)
 - **Calling for backup**: When falling below 25% health, the Shaman enters stasis for 10 seconds (invulnerable and stunned). While in stasis, the Shaman spawns 4 Elite enemies.
 - **Even in death**: When defeated, the Shaman deals 20% of the player's max HP as damage and increases all surviving enemies' physical damage by 15% and magic damage by 10% for 15 seconds.

#### Magician

The Magician is an enemy type that leverages instant mobility to close distance and deal heavy damage.

##### Abilities

 - **Blink**: Charges up for a brief windup, then instantly teleports on top of the player, dealing medium damage. (short cooldown)
 - **Where are you going?**: Fires a projectile toward the player. If it hits, heavy damage is dealt and the player is pulled toward the Magician for 2 seconds at 800 movement speed. (long cooldown)
 - **There's no escape**: When the Magician is close to the player (<400 distance), the player is slowed until moving out of range. The slow magnitude scales with the room number (round).
 - **Covered attack**: Fires 3 projectiles (-15°, 0°, +15°) that stun the player on hit for 1 second and deal massive damage. Only triggers when the player is close to the Magician (<400 distance). (medium cooldown)

#### Lifeliner

The Lifeliner saves other enemies from certain death by moving them out of harm's way.
The Lifeliner has very high attack speed and high movement speed; by its nature, it prefers staying at range from the player.

##### Abilities

 - **You won't die today**: Teleports to a low-HP ally (<10% HP) and moves them to safety (at least 1000 units away from the player). (short cooldown)
 - **You did not die**: Randomly revives a fallen enemy back into the arena. The revived enemy starts with 33% HP, which scales with rooms completed (rounds). (long cooldown)

#### Angler

The Angler is an enemy type with very high attack speed and multi-directional attacks. It does not have specialized spells, but it locks down cardinal axes with projectiles. It fires at angles 0°, 90°, -90°, and 180° with an attack speed of 10 (scaling with room number) or higher and no additional cooldown. Each projectile deals low damage (<10).

#### Tank

The Tank is a high-HP, low-damage, crowd-control-focused enemy.

##### Abilities

 - **Root on hit**: While active, whenever the player attacks the Tank, the player is rooted for 1 second (scaling with room level). Displays an aura visual effect so the player knows the reactive shield is active. (long cooldown)
 - **Slow attack**: Fires a burst of projectiles, each stacking a slow effect on the player by a set amount. (short cooldown)
 - **Knockback**: Fires a projectile that knocks the player back. (short cooldown)
 - **Stun attack**: Fires an eight-directional burst of projectiles (0°, 45°, -45°, 90°, -90°, 135°, -135°, 180°) that stun the player on hit. (medium cooldown)

### Mini-Bosses

Mini-bosses are high-HP, high-damage encounters designed to provide a greater challenge than standard rooms.
Their health is displayed in a dedicated boss health bar at the top of the screen instead of floating above them.

#### Knight

The Knight prefers close-range combat and heavily punishes the player for moving out of melee range.

##### Passive Abilities

 - **Your attacks don't affect me**: When the player is far (>400 distance) from the Knight, the Knight gains Armour & MR scaling with distance along a parabolic curve—capping at 10,000 Armour & MR at 1000 distance.
 - **Drain**: When the player is out of range (>400 distance), they suffer continuous damage scaling with distance—starting at 1 dmg/s at 401 distance and reaching 15 dmg/s at 1000 distance (scaling with room level). The Knight is healed for the full amount of damage dealt.

##### Abilities

 - **I'll strike you down**: Long-windup, high-damage, close-range attack. (low cooldown)
 - **Beheading**: Long-windup, stun-inflicting attack that deals % max HP damage starting at 15% and scaling with room level. (medium cooldown)
 - **Weaken**: Periodically applies a self-stun for several seconds. While weakened, the Knight loses 50% Armour & MR. After the stun clears, its Armour & MR increase by +15 relative to their values before Weaken was triggered. (long cooldown)
 - **I will not die**: When falling below 25% health, the Knight stuns itself and channels healing for 5 seconds at a base rate of 5% max HP/s, scaling with rooms completed (rounds).

#### Bishop

The Bishop does not aim directly at the player, but instead attacks in continuous sweeping radial patterns.

##### Abilities

 - **Rotating streak (knockback)**: Fires in 4 orthogonal directions (0°, 90°, -90°, 180°) with a continuously rotating heading. Each projectile deals low damage and knocks back the player. Rather than tracking the player, it sweeps in a full circle, firing a projectile every 0.5 degrees. Total cast duration is 5 seconds. All projectiles travel at 750 speed and last 5 seconds. (low cooldown)
 - **Rotating streak (slow)**: Fires in 4 orthogonal directions (0°, 90°, -90°, 180°) with a rotating heading. Each projectile deals low damage and slows the player (scaling with rooms completed). Fires every 0.5 degrees across a 5-second cast duration. Projectiles travel at 750 speed and last 5 seconds. (medium cooldown)
 - **Rotating streak (root)**: Fires in 4 orthogonal directions (0°, 90°, -90°, 180°) with a rotating heading. Each projectile deals low damage and roots the player for 0.5 seconds (scaling with rooms completed). Fires every 0.5 degrees across a 5-second cast duration. Projectiles travel at 750 speed and last 5 seconds. (high cooldown)
 - **Rotating streak (stun)**: Fires in 4 orthogonal directions (0°, 90°, -90°, 180°) with a rotating heading. Each projectile deals low damage and stuns the player for 0.5 seconds (scaling with rooms completed). Fires every 0.5 degrees across a 5-second cast duration. Projectiles travel at 750 speed and last 5 seconds. (extra high cooldown)

### Bosses

Bosses have massive HP pools, high durability, high movement speed, and devastating damage.
Their health is displayed in a dedicated boss health bar at the top of the screen instead of floating above them.

#### The King

The King prefers commanding other enemies rather than fighting directly, but fights as a dangerous melee brawler when engaged. The King's attacks are wide and always fire multiple projectiles simultaneously.

##### Abilities (above 50% health)

- **I will not fight a peasant (mini-boss)**: Summons two random mini-bosses and enters stasis while they are alive. After both mini-bosses are defeated, he immediately casts Weaken.
- **I will not fight a peasant (normal)**: Summons regular enemies (starting at 40 and scaling with rooms completed) and enters stasis while they are alive. After all summoned enemies are defeated, he immediately casts Weaken.
- **Weaken**: Self-inflicts a stun for several seconds; while weakened, the King loses 50% Armour & MR.

##### Abilities (under 50% health)

- **Apply root**: Roots the player for 1.5 seconds.
- **Apply slow**: Slows the player for 1.5 seconds (effectiveness scales with rooms completed).

#### The Queen

> [!NOTE]
> **Bond of Life** 
> When Bond of Life is applied, the next attack deals % max HP true damage (bypassing Armour and MR) and heals the caster. However, if the attack misses, the caster takes the damage instead and the effect is removed from the target.

The Queen is a high-risk opponent who periodically applies Bond of Life.

##### Abilities

- **Bond of Life: Apply**: Applies Bond of Life to the player at 10% effectiveness (10% max HP true damage on hit). Can only be cast when Bond of Life is not currently active on the player.
- **Pop Bond of Life (radial)**: Attacks in 8 directions (0°, 45°, -45°, 90°, -90°, 135°, -135°, 180°) with a rotating heading. If any projectile hits, it pops Bond of Life and cancels the remainder of the attack. If the player avoids all projectiles, Bond of Life expires and The Queen takes the damage. Projectiles travel at 750 speed and last 5 seconds. (Can only be cast while Bond of Life is active)
- **Pop Bond of Life (dash attack)**: Dashes toward the player while firing 3 waves of wide projectiles. If any projectile hits, it pops Bond of Life and cancels the attack. If the player avoids all projectiles, Bond of Life expires and The Queen takes the damage. (Can only be cast while Bond of Life is active)
- **Pop Bond of Life (sniper)**: Fires a fast-traveling projectile toward the player. If it hits, it pops Bond of Life and stuns the player for 2 seconds. If the player avoids the projectile, Bond of Life expires and The Queen takes the damage. (Can only be cast while Bond of Life is active)

## Tutorial

The first room of every run will be the tutorial room. It features a free boon selection at the start and no enemies, only a training dummy. 

### Objectives

The player receives introductory objectives teaching core actions:
- Move with WASD
- Dash with Space
- Light attack
- Heavy attack
- Dash attack
- Interact with the boon
- Interact with the door

These objectives should only be shown once, when the player first starts playing the game – the save file must include it. If the player have already completed the tutorial, there shall be a "Tutorial" button in the Main Menu.

## Tile-Based Background for Rooms/Maps

We will modernize and integrate the existing tile-based map system to create a richer visual experience. This includes an integrated map editor and persistent map save files.
